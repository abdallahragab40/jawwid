= Ash-Shatibiyyah | a poem whose ryhme is laam of the seven recitations by Imam Abi Al-Qasim Ibn Firuh Al-Andalusi. It is called (**Hirz al-Amani wa Wajh at-Tahani**); It contains the book of A' taiseer for Abi Amr Ad'ani, consisting of 1173 verses.
= Ad-Durrah (Ad-Durrah al-Mudiyyah fil-Qira'at ath-Thalath al-Mutammimah lil-'Ashr al-Mardiyyah) | a poem in the science of recitations, collecting the three recitations that complement the ten: (Abu-Ja'far al-Madani, Ya'qub al-Basri and khalaf al-kufi) for the Imam Mohamed Ibn Al-Gazari. The number of its verses.
= Tayyibat an-Nashr | a poem of the ten recitations for the Imam Mohamed Ibn Al-Gazari. It contains his book annshr fil-Qira'at Al-Ashr, consisting of 1015 verses.
