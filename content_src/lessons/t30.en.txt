= Ash-Shatibiyyah | A poem rhyming in laam on the seven recitations, by Imam Abu al-Qasim ibn Firruh al-Andalusi. It is called (**Hirz al-Amani wa Wajh at-Tahani**); in it he versified the book At-Taysir by Abu 'Amr ad-Dani, in 1173 verses.
= Ad-Durrah (Ad-Durrah al-Mudiyyah fil-Qira'at ath-Thalath al-Mutammimah lil-'Ashr al-Mardiyyah) | A poem in the science of recitations, collecting the three recitations that complete the ten (Abu Ja'far al-Madani, Ya'qub al-Basri and Khalaf al-Kufi), by Imam Muhammad ibn al-Jazari; it has 240 verses.
= Tayyibat an-Nashr | A poem on the ten recitations by Imam Muhammad ibn al-Jazari, in which he versified his book An-Nashr fil-Qira'at al-'Ashr, in 1015 verses.
