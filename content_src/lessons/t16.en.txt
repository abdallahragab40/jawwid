# The strongest madd and using the rule of the stronger of two causes ({{a أقوى المُدُودِ والعملُ بقاعدةِ أقوى السَّببين}})
- If two causes are met in a lengthening letter, one of them is strong and the other is weak so, the stronger is used and the weaker is neglected.
- Secondary lengthening appear in 5 ranks according to strength and weakness. They will be ordered from the stronger to the weaker as follows. Sheikh As-Samannudi says:
> {{a أقْوَى المُدُودِ: لَازِمٌ فَمَا اتَّصَلْ ... فَعَارِضٌ فَذُو انفِصَالٍ فَبَدَلْ}}
> {{a وَسَبَبَا مَدٍّ إذا مَا وُجِدَا ... فَإِنَّ أقْوَى السَّبَبَيْنِ انْفَرَدَا}}
The Strongest of lengthenings is **Lazim** (Compulsory (1) and then **Muttasil** (joined) (2), **'Arid lis-sukoon** (conditional because of sukoon) (3), **Munfasil** (Separate (4) and then **Badal** (exchange) (5).
| Madd | Duration (counts) |
| 1 – Lazim | 6 |
| 2 – Muttasil | 4 – 5 |
| 3 – 'Arid lis-sukoon | 2 – 4 – 6 |
| 4 – Munfasil | 4 – 5 |
| 5 – Badal | 2 |

## Meeting lengthenings in the rule of the stronger of two causes ({{a المُدودُ التي تَجتَمعُ في قاعدةِ أقوى السَّبَبَين}})
1) **Meeting Lazim with Badal**, like: {{q 5:2|ءَ[آ]مِّينَ}}, {{q ?|ءَ[آ]للَّهُ}}, {{q 10:91|ءَ[آ]لْـَٰٔنَ}}, {{q ?|ءَ[آ]لذَّكَرَيْنِ}}. **Lazim** is used first.
2) **Meeting Muttasil with Badal**, like: {{q ?|رِئَ[آءَ] ٱلنَّاسِ}}. **Muttasil** is used first.
3) **Meeting 'Arid lis-sukoon with Badal**, like: {{q ?|شَنَـَٔ[ا]نُ}}, {{q ?|يُرَآءُ[و]نَ}}, {{q ?|خَٰسِـِٔ[ي]نَ}}. **'Arid lis-sukoon** is used first.
4) **Meeting Munfasil with Badal**, like: {{q 12:16|وَجَآءُ[وٓ أَ]بَاهُمْ}}. **Munfasil** is used first.
5) **Meeting Muttasil, Badal and 'Arid lis-sukoon**, like: {{q ?|رِئَ[آءَ]}}. Badal is neglected because it is weak, so the remaining lengthenings (Mu'tasil and Arid li'sukoon) subject to the same rules.
6) **Meeting Muttasil with 'Arid lis-sukoon**, like: {{q ?|ٱلسَّمَ[آءُ]}}.
