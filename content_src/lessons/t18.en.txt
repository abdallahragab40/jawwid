# Rules of the non-voweled laam ({{a أحكامُ اللَّامِ السَّاكِنةِ}})
The Non-Voweled Laams in the Noble Qur'an are of five types:
## 1- Laam of the definite article ({{a لامُ التَّعريف (ال)}})
It is an extra laam sakinah added to the basic makeup of the word, preceded by linking hamzah (hamzatul-wasl) with Fathah when starting and followed by noun.
### Laam qamariyyah (lunar laam)
It is followed by any of the letters of (**ابْغِ حَجَّكَ وَخَفْ عَقِيمَه**).
= Its rule | Ithhar (clarity).
= Its sign in the Qur'an | A sukoon sign above the laam.

### Laam shamsiyyah (solar laam)
It is followed by any of initials of each word in the following verse:
> {{a طِبْ ثُمَّ صِلْ رُحمًا تَفُزْ ضِفْ ذَا نِعَمْ ... دَعْ سُوءَ ظَنٍّ زُرْ شَرِيفًا لِلكَرَمْ}}
= Its rule | Idgham (merging).
= Its sign in the Qur'an | Laam is devoid of sukoon sign and shaddah is drawn above the next letter.

## 2- Laam of the noun (laam al-ism) ({{a لامُ الاسم}})
It is a permanent laam in the root of the noun and always comes in the middle of the word.
= Its rule | Ithhar (clarity).

## 3- Laam of command (laam al-amr) ({{a لامُ الأمرِ}})
It is an extra non-voweled laam, added to the present-tense verb and changes it into the imperative form. It is usually preceded by a particle such as faa ({{a فـ}}), waaw ({{a و}}) or thumma ({{a ثُمَّ}}).
= Its rule | Ithhar (clarity).
| | | | |
| {{q ?|فَ[لْ]يَمْدُدْ}} | {{q ?|وَ[لْ]يُوفُوا۟}} | {{q ?|وَ[لْ]يَطَّوَّفُوا۟}} | {{q ?|ثُمَّ [لْ]يَقْطَعْ}} |
## 4- Laam of the verb (laam al-fi'l) ({{a لامُ الفعل}})
It is the original non-voweled laam that appears in verbs, whether past, present or imperative, in the middle of the word or at its end.
- **Past tense**: {{q ?|أَرْسَ[لْ]نَا}}, **present tense**: {{q ?|يَ[لْ]تَقِطْهُ}}, **imperative**: {{q ?|وَأَ[لْ]قِ}}.
= Its rule | Ithhar (Clarity). But, If Laam of verb is at the end and followed by the letter **laam** or **raa**, idgham (merging) is required.

## 5- Laam of the particle (laam al-harf) ({{a لامُ الحرف}})
This refers to the laam that only occurs in the particles (**هل**) and (**بل**); These are the only two instances of laam al-harf found in the Noble Qur'an.
= Its rule | Idgham (merging) If the laam is followed by **laam** or **raa**, like:
| | | | |
| {{q 30:28|هَ[ل لَّ]كُم مِّن مَّا مَلَكَتْ}} | {{q 3:154|هَ[ل لَّ]نَا مِنَ ٱلْأَمْرِ}} | {{q 4:158|بَ[ل رَّ]فَعَهُ ٱللَّهُ}} | {{q 21:56|بَ[ل رَّ]بُّكُمْ}} |
### Note
1) It is excluded from this rule: {{q 83:14|بَ[لْ رَ]انَ}} [Al-Mutaffifin: 14], where there is sakt (slight pause without taking breath), preventing idgham.
2) Know that raa never occurs after (**هل**) in the Qur'an.
= Its rule | clarity (Ithhar)If the laam is followed by any other letter, it is pronounced clearly b. Examples include:
| | | | |
| {{q ?|هَ[لْ] نَحْنُ}} | {{q ?|هَ[لْ] يَسْتَطِيعُ}} | {{q ?|بَ[لْ] طَبَعَ}} | {{q ?|بَ[لْ] هُمْ}} |
- **Briefly**: a sukoon is placed on the non-voweled laam in the Qur'an to show that it is pronounced clearly (ithhar), like: {{q ?|ٱ[لْ]جَنَّةَ}}, {{q ?|سَ[لْ]سَبِيلًا}}, {{q ?|أَرْسَ[لْ]نَا}}, {{q ?|فَ[لْ]يَمْدُدْ}}.
