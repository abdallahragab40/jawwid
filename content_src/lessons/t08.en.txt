# Characteristics of letters ({{a صِفاتُ الحُروف}})
### Definition of characteristic
= In linguistics | Describing something with physical or abstract meanings.
= In applied definition | A way is used to describe the letter when it occurs at its articulation point.
### Benefits of knowing characteristics
1) **To distinguish among letters that share the same articulation point**: If these letters don't have different characteristics, they will be the same letter. If there is no isti'laa or itbaq (elevation or adhesion) with s'aad, it will be seen and with no jahr (appearence) with zaay, it will be seen.
2) **To beautify the pronunciation of the letter**: Giving every letter its required characteristics makes the pronunciation more fluent, perfect and superior, whereas neglecting the characteristics makes the letters overlapped and similar or not clear in pronunciation.
3) **To know strong and weak letters**: The letter that is characterized by strong qualities, is strong and the letter that is characterized by weak qualities is weak, The letter also may have both strong and weak qualities at the same time so, we judge upon it according to the majority of its characteristics.

@page 26
### Divisions of characteristics ({{a أقسامُ الصِّفات}})
1) **Permanent characteristics** ({{a صفاتٌ لازمَةٌ}}): They always accompany the letter in all cases, but they may not appear clearly when pronouncing the letter For example : qalqalah and hams (Vibration and whisper) only appear when pronouncing non-voweled letter.
2) **Temporary characteristics** ({{a صفاتٌ عارضةٌ}}): They do not accompany the letter except in certain cases for a reason and they will be removed if the reason is removed.
Temporary characteristics are 11:
- Idgham (merging) ({{a الإدغام}}).
- Ithhar (clarity) ({{a الإظهار}}).
- Qalb (converting) ({{a القَلْب}}).
- Ikhfaa (hiding) ({{a الإخفاء}}).
- Madd (lenghtening) ({{a المَدّ}}).
- Qasr (shortening) ({{a القَصر}}).
- Tahrik (with a vowel) ({{a التَّحريك}}).
- Sukoon (with a sukoon) ({{a السُّكون}}).
- Sakt (slight pause without breathing) ({{a السَّكت}}).
- Tafkheem (heaviness) ({{a التَّفخيم}}).
- Tarqiq (lightness) ({{a التَّرقيق}}).
### Number of permanent characteristics ({{a عددُ الصِّفاتِ اللَّازمة}})
Scholars have different opinions about the number of characteristics of the letters, The majority of scholars and Ibn Al-Gazari chose in the introduction of Al-Gazariah and Taybat Annshr that they are **17 characteristics**, and this is the common opinion.

# 1- Hams VS Jahr ({{a الهَمسُ وضِدُّهُ الجَهرُ}})
## Hams
= In linguistics | Whisper.
= In applied definition | continuation of breath when pronouncing the letter because of its weakness that originated from depending on the letter at its articulation point.
= Its letters | 10 letters are collected by Ibn Al-Gazari in the phrase ({{a فَحَثَّهُ شَخْصٌ سَكَتْ}}).
= The reason for this name | Its letters are called (Whispered) because they are articulated by a concealed sound.
= Examples | {{q ?|أَ[فْ]وَاجًا}}, {{q ?|ٱلرَّ[حْ]مَٰنُ}}, {{q ?|مِ[ثْ]قَالَ}}, {{q ?|ٱ[هْ]دِنَا}}, {{q ?|ٱ[شْ]تَرَوُا۟}}, {{q ?|سَيَ[صْ]لَىٰ}}, {{q ?|ٱ[سْ]كُنْ}}, {{q ?|أَ[كْ]بَرُ}}, {{q ?|كُوِّرَ[تْ]}}.
## Jahr
= In linguistics | To be apparent.
= In applied definition | discontinuation of breath when pronouncing the letter because of its strength that originated from depending on the letter at its articulation point.
= Its letters | 19: The rest of the Arabic alphabet except letters of hams.
= The reason for this name | Its letters are called apparent because they are articulated by a strong and audible sound.
= Examples | {{q ?|تَ[قْ]رَبَا}}, {{q ?|ٱلْعِ[جْ]لَ}}, {{q ?|ٱ[ذْ]كُرُوا۟}}, {{q ?|ٱلْأَ[رْ]ضِ}}, {{q ?|عَ[دْ]لٌ}}.
### The difference between breath ({{a النَّفَس}}) and sound ({{a الصَّوت}})
- **Breath**: the air that is emitted from the lungs by nature (inhale and exhale).
- **Sound**: The air that is emitted from lungs, causing vibration as a result of the colliding of two objects or narrowing or closing the passage completely and after that releasing it.

= In linguistics | Moderation.
= In applied definition | The sound is moderate between Shiddah and Rakhawah when pronouncing its letters because It's partially (imprisoned) as in Shiddah and partially (running) as in Rakhawah.

# Measure of timings of letters
- **Voweled letters**: the timings of voweled letters are equal, like: {{q ?|كُتُبٌ}}.
- **Non-voweled letters**: the timing of a **soft** letter is longer than that of a **moderate** letter, and the timing of a **moderate** letter is also longer than that of a **strong** letter.

## Ist'laa
= In linguistics | Raising and elevating.
= In applied definition | Raising the furthest part of the tongue to the roof of the mouth when pronouncing the letter so, the sound is also raised.

# 4- It'baq VS Infitah ({{a الإطباقُ وضِدُّهُ الإنفتاح}})
## It'baq
= In linguistics | Adhesion.
= In applied definition | compression of the sound of the letter between the tongue and the roof of the mouth when pronouncing the letter.
= Its letters | 4 letters ({{a ص، ض، ط، ظ}}).
= Examples | {{q ?|أَ[صْ]حَٰبُ}}, {{q ?|فَ[ضْ]لِ}}, {{q ?|شَ[طْ]رَ}}, {{q ?|أَ[ظْ]لَمُ}}.

## Infitah
= In linguistics | Separation.
= In applied definition | Absence of the compression of the sound as a result of Separating between the tongue and the roof of the mouth.
= Its letters | the rest of the alphabetical letters except letters of It'baq.
= Examples | {{q ?|أَ[شْ]تَاتًا}}, {{q ?|ٱلْأَ[سْ]وَدِ}}, {{q ?|بَ[عَثْ]نَا}}.

# 5- Ithlaq VS Is'mat ({{a الإذلاقُ وضِدُّهُ الإصمات}})
## Ithlaq
= In linguistics | Eloquence and fluency of the tongue.
= In applied definition | Light and quick pronunciation of the letter because it is emitted from the tip of the tongue or the lips.
= Its letters | 6 letters collected by the regulator in the phrase ({{a فِرَّ مِنْ لُبِّ}}).
= Examples | {{q ?|تَ[فْ]عَلُوا۟}}, {{q ?|أَ[بْ]صَٰرِهِمْ}}.
## Is'mat
= In linguistics | Prevention.
= In applied definition | Heavy and difficult pronunciation of the letter because its articulation point is away from the tip of the tongue and the lips.
= Its letters | the rest of the letters except letters of Ithlaq.
> **Note**: These two characteristics are derived from morphology and they have no influence on pronunciation.

# Characteristics with no opposites ({{a صفاتٌ ليسَ لها ضِدّ}})
## 1- S'afir
= In linguistics | A whistle that looks like the sound of bird.
= In applied definition | A Whistle accompanies letters of Safir that refers to its sharp sound.
= Its letters | 3 letters ({{a ص، ز، س}}).
= It is called by this name | Because when letters of safir are pronounced, It can be heard a sound like the whistle of bird.
= Examples | {{q ?|ٱلْفَ[صْ]لِ}}, {{q ?|وَٱ[صْ]فَحُوا۟}}, {{q ?|رِ[زْ]قَهُ}}, {{q ?|بِٱلْهَ[زْ]لِ}}, {{q ?|يَ[سْ]مَعُونَ}}.
> **Note**: Amongst common mistakes when pronouncing S'aad is circling lips, Lips do not play any role in emitting S'aad neither from the articulation point nor the characteristic.
## 2- Qalqalah
= In linguistics | Disturbance.
= In applied definition | The letter is disturbed at its articulation point when it is pronounced with Sukoon, producing a strong tone.
= Its letters | 5 letters collected in ({{a قُطْبُ جَدٍّ}}).
= Examples | {{q ?|ٱلْحَ[قُّ]}}, {{q ?|أَ[طْ]عَمَهُم}}, {{q ?|وَتَ[بَّ]}}, {{q ?|نَ[جْ]عَل}}, {{q ?|لَمْ يَلِ[دْ]}}.
### Qalqalah has 3 ranks
1) **Major qalqalah**: When the letter of qalqalah comes at the end of the word with shaddah sign and we stop on it: {{q ?|ٱلْحَ[قُّ]}}, {{q ?|وَتَ[بَّ]}}, {{q ?|ٱلْحَ[جَّ]}}, {{q ?|أَ[شَدُّ]}}.
2) **Medium qalqalah**: When the letter of qalqalah comes at the end of the word with no shaddah sign and we stop on it: {{q ?|تَخْلُ[قُ]}}, {{q ?|بِٱلْقِسْ[طِ]}}, {{q 26:63|ٱضْرِ[ب]}}, {{q ?|بَهِي[جٍ]}}, {{q ?|يُولَ[دْ]}}.
3) **Minor qalqalah**: When the letter of qalqalah comes in the middle of the word or words with sukoon sign: {{q ?|نَ[قْ]عًا}}, {{q ?|أَ[طْ]عَمَهُم}}, {{q ?|حَ[بْ]لٌ}}, {{q ?|نَ[جْ]عَل}}, {{q ?|يَ[دْ]خُلُونَ}}, {{q 89:8|يُخْلَ[قْ] مِثْلُهَا}}, {{q ?|تُسَٰ[قِطْ] عَلَيْكِ}}, {{q ?|ٱذْهَ[بْ] إِلَىٰ}}, {{q ?|تَخْرُ[جْ] بَيْضَآءَ}}, {{q ?|قَ[دْ] أَفْلَحَ}}.
> **Note**: Qalqalah is not applied to the merged letter, like: {{q ?|ٱضْرِ[ب] بِّعَصَاكَ}}, {{q ?|وَقَ[د] دَّخَلُوا۟}}, {{q ?|قَ[د] تَّبَيَّنَ}}, {{q ?|بَسَ[طت]}}, {{q 12:80|فَرَّ[طت]مْ}}.

## 5- Takrir
= In linguistics | Repetition.
= In applied definition | The trilling of the tip of the tongue when pronouncing the letter.
= Its letters | only letter ({{a ر}}).
= It is called by this name | Because the tip of the tongue trills when pronouncing the letter.

## 6- Tafashy
= In linguistics | Spreading out.
= In applied definition | Spreading out the breath through the mouth when pronouncing the letter until it reaches the inner plates of the upper incisors.
= Its letters | only letter ({{a ش}}).
= It is called by this name | Because the breath is spread out through the mouth when pronouncing the letter.
= Examples | {{q ?|نَ[شْ]هَدُ}}, {{q ?|وَأَ[شْ]هِدُوا۟}}, {{q ?|تَ[شْ]كُرُونَ}}.
## 7- Istitalah
= In linguistics | Lengthening.
= In applied definition | Lengthening the sound of d'aad at its articulation point from the edge of the tongue (from the back to the front) until it touches the articulation point of laam (where the tip of the tongue slightly touches the gum of the central incisors, lateral incisors, canines and premolars).
= Its letters | only letter ({{a ض}}).
= It is called by this name | Because its letter is lengthened at its articulation point from the back part of the edge of the tongue to its tip.
= Examples | {{q ?|ٱل[ضَّ]آلِّينَ}}, {{q ?|أَ[ضْ]عَفُ}}, {{q ?|ٱلْأَرْ[ضِ]}}.
> **Note**: Some scholars added another characteristics of the letters, , highlighting ghunnah (nasalization) with (meem and noon), We will explain it in the subject of timings of letters in Page 50.

# Strong and weak characteristics ({{a الصِّفاتُ القويَّةُ والصِّفاتُ الضَّعيفةُ}})
According to strength and weakness, characteristics can be divided into 3 sections:
= Strong characteristics | They are 11: Jahr, Shiddah, Isti'laa, It'baq, Safir, Qalqalah, Inhiraf, Takrir, Tafashy, Istitalah and Ghunnah (Appearence, strength, elevation, adhesion, whistle, disturbance, deviation, Repitition, spreading out, lengthening and nasalization).
= Weak characteristics | They are 6: Hams, Rakhawah, Istifaal, Infitah, Leen and Khafaa (whisper, softness, lowering, Separating, ease and hiding).
= Characteristics that are neither strong nor weak | They are 3: Is'mat, Ithlaq and Tawassut (prevention, fluency and moderation).
- According to strength and weakness, Letters can be divided into 5 sections:
1) **Strongest**: All its characteristics are strong.
2) **Strong**: Most its characteristics are strong.
3) **Moderate**: Its strong characteristics are equal to the weak characteristics.
4) **Weak**: Most its characteristics are weak.
5) **Weakest**: All its characteristics are weak.
### Question: How do you find out the characteristics of a letter?
**Answer**:
1) For the characteristics with Opposites: Try to find one of them otherwise, the letter is characterized by one of their opposites.
2) Then, For the characteristics with no opposites: IF the letter is characterized by one of them, we count this characteristic added to the five previous ones.
3) And then, We look at the degree of the letter through its characteristics and according to this, It will be strong or weak.
### Note
1) Any of the alphabetical letters must be characterized by five of the characteristics with opposites, and then it may have one, two or none of the characteristics with no opposites.
2) The single letter has 7 characteristics as maximum and have 5 characteristics as minimum.
