# The most prominent mistakes when pronouncing the letters of the alphabet ({{a أبرزُ الأخطاءِ عِندَ نُطقِ حروفِ الهجاءِ}})
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Alif
1) Not opening mouth as required like: {{q ?|مُوسَ[ىٰ]}}.
2) Making it heavy where it should be light like: {{q ?|ٱلنَّهَ[ا]رَ}}, {{q ?|ٱلنَّ[ا]رِ}}.
3) Making it light where it should be heavy like: {{q ?|[خَٰ]لِدِينَ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Hamzah ({{a ء}})
1) Making it heavy like: {{q ?|[أَ]صَابَهَا}}.
2) Making it weak (by not applying nabr [stress]) when stopping like: {{q ?|ٱلسَّمَآ[ءِ]}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Haa ({{a هـ}})
1) Making it heavy like: {{q ?|ٱلنَّ[هَ]ارِ}}.
2) Weakening the sound of haa like: {{q ?|ٱ[هْ]دِنَا}}.
3) Weakening its sound (by not applying hams [whisper]) when stopping like: {{q ?|مِّن مِّثْلِ[هِۦ]}}, {{q ?|مَّا فَعَلُو[هُ]}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Ain ({{a ع}})
1) Cutting off the sound of ain when it comes sakinah like: {{q ?|يَ[عْ]لَمُونَ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Haa ({{a ح}})
1) Weakening the characteristic of hams like: {{q ?|ٱلرَّ[حْ]مَٰنِ ٱلرَّ[حِ]يمِ}}.
2) Replacing it with haa ({{a هـ}}), like: {{q ?|ٱلْ[حَ]مْدُ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Ghain ({{a غ}})
1) Exachanging it into khaa, like: {{q ?|يَ[غْ]شَىٰ}}.
2) Applying qalqalah like: {{q ?|ٱلْمَ[غْ]ضُوبِ}}.

### Mistakes made when pronouncing Qaaf ({{a ق}})
1) Not applying qalqalah like: {{q ?|ٱلْحَ[قُّ]}}.
2) Mixing its sound with a hint of the sound of kaaf like: {{q ?|[قَ]الُوا۟}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Kaaf ({{a ك}})
1) Not applying hams (whisper) especially when it is sakinah like: {{q ?|أَ[كْ]بَرُ}}, {{q ?|صَدْرَ[كَ]}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Jeem ({{a ج}})
1) Not applying qalqalah like: {{q ?|أَ[جْ]رُهُمْ}}, {{q ?|ٱلْحَ[جَّ]}}.
2) Pronouncing it with Rakhawah (softness) like: {{q ?|[جَ]عَلُوا۟}}, {{q ?|وَ[جَ]نَّةٍ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Sheen ({{a ش}})
1) Weakening the sound of Sheen like: {{q ?|أَ[شْ]تَاتًا}}, {{q ?|وَأَ[شْ]هِدُوا۟}}.
2) Making it heavy when it is adjacent to a heavy letter like: {{q ?|[شَ]طَطًا}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Yaa ({{a ي}})
1) Mixing its sound with a hint of the sound of ghunnah (nasalization) like: {{q ?|ٱلْعَٰلَمِ[ي]نَ}}, {{q ?|ٱلْحَٰكِمِ[ي]نَ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing D'aad ({{a ض}})
1) Converting it into th'aa like: {{q ?|[ضَ]لَّ}}, {{q ?|[ضَ]لَٰلٍ}}.
2) Converting it into daal like: {{q ?|تُفِي[ضُ]ونَ}}.
3) Applying qalqalah like: {{q ?|وَعِنَبًا وَقَ[ضْ]بًا}}.
4) Merging it into the following letter like: {{q ?|ٱ[ضْ]طُرَّ}}, {{q ?|عَرَّ[ضْ]تُم}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Laam ({{a ل}})
1) Making it heavy where it should be light like: {{q ?|تَعْ[لَ]مُونَ}}, {{q ?|زُ[لْ]زِلَتِ}}.
2) Making it light where it should be heavy like: {{q ?|عَبْدُ [ٱللَّهِ]}}, {{q ?|عِبَادَ [ٱللَّهِ]}}.
3) Merging it into the next letter (not pronounced clearly) like: {{q ?|وَجَعَ[لْ]نَا}}.
4) Merging laam qamaryyiah (lunar laam) into Jeem like: {{q ?|ٱ[لْ]جَنَّةِ}}, {{q ?|ٱ[لْ]جِبَالُ}}.

### Mistakes made when pronouncing Noon ({{a ن}})
1) Not giving its right of ghunnah when stopping like: {{q ?|ٱلْعَٰلَمِي[نَ]}}.
2) Vibrating (Tant'neen) the sound of ghunnah when noon comes with shaddah like: {{q ?|مِنَ ٱلْجِ[نَّ]ةِ وَٱل[نَّ]اسِ}}, by waving and trembling its sound when articulating it.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Raa ({{a ر}})
1) Making it heavy where it should be light like: {{q ?|فِ[رْ]عَوْنَ}}.
2) Making it light where it should be heavy like: {{q ?|مَ[رْ]يَمَ}}, {{q ?|ٱلْقُ[رْ]ءَانُ}}.
3) Exaggrating repitition of raa if it is mushaddadah or sakinah like: {{q ?|إِنِ ٱ[رْ]تَبْتُمْ}}.
4) Not pronouncing clearly when it is sakinah due to stopping like: {{q ?|نَصِي[رٍ]}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing T'aa ({{a ط}})
1) Making it light like: {{q ?|[طِ]بَاقًا}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Daal ({{a د}})
1) Mixing its sound with a hint of a sound taa like: {{q ?|ٱل[دِّ]ينِ}}.
2) Making it heavy like: {{q ?|صُ[دُ]ورِ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Taa ({{a ت}})
1) Exaggrating hams of taa when it is voweled, like: {{q ?|[تَ]تَفَكَّرُونَ}}.
2) Making it heavy like: {{q ?|[تَ]طْمَئِنُّ}}.
3) Not applying hams especially when it is sakinah like: {{q ?|طَلَعَ[ت]}}, {{q ?|غَرَبَ[ت]}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing the letters of Safir (whistle) ({{a ص، ز، س}})
1) Weakening the characteristic of Safir (whistle) like: {{q ?|ٱل[صَّ]مَدُ}}, {{q ?|ٱلْمِ[سْ]كِينِ}}, {{q ?|يَ[زَّ]كَّىٰ}}.
2) Circling lips when pronouncing S'aad like: {{q ?|ٱل[صَّ]ٰلِحَٰتِ}}.
3) Making S'aad light like: {{q ?|ٱلْمَ[صِ]يرُ}}.
4) Making seen heavy like: {{q ?|يَ[سْ]طُرُونَ}}.
5) Mixing the sound of seen with a hint of the sound of zaay like: {{q ?|رِجْ[سٌ]}}.

### Mistakes made when pronouncing the gum letters ({{a ظ، ذ، ث}})
1) Putting the tip of the tongue out of the mouth more than necessary, like: {{q ?|وَٱل[ظَّ]ٰلِمِينَ}}.
2) Placing the tip of the tongue upon gum or the inner plate of upper incisors.
3) Changing thaal into zaay like: {{q ?|وَٱل[ذَّ]ٰكِرِينَ}}.
4) Changing thaa into seen or taa like: {{q ?|فَكَ[ثَّ]رَكُمْ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Faa ({{a ف}})
1) Not pronouncing clearly because of weak hams (Whisper) like: {{q ?|تَ[فْ]عَلُونَ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Waaw ({{a و}})
1) Not circling lips as required like: {{q ?|[وَ]ٱللَّهُ}}, {{q ?|ٱلْمُفْلِحُ[و]نَ}}.
2) Mixing its sound with a hint of the sound of ghunnah like: {{q ?|يُوقِنُ[و]نَ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Baa ({{a ب}})
1) Making it heavy when it is adjacent to heavy letter like: {{q ?|ٱلْ[بَ]صَرَ}}, {{q ?|بِٱلْ[بَ]ٰطِلِ}}.
2) Not applying qalqalah when it comes with Sukoon like: {{q ?|وَيُ[بْ]صِرُونَ}}, {{q ?|وَيُ[بْ]طِلَ}}.
### Mistakes made when pronouncing Meem ({{a م}})
1) Cutting off its sound when stopping like: {{q ?|ٱلرَّحِي[مِ]}}, {{q ?|ٱلْحَكِي[مُ]}}.
2) Making it heavy when it is adjacent to heavy letter like: {{q ?|[مَ]خْ[مَ]صَةٍ}}.
3) Doing qalqalah when meem comes with Sukoon like: {{q ?|يَ[مْ]تَرُونَ}}, {{q ?|يُ[مْ]سِكُهُنَّ}}.
