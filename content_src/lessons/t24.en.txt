# Nabr (accent) in the Qur'an ({{a النَّبرُ في القرآنِ الكريم}})
## Its definition
= In linguistics | Pressing and raising the voice.
= In applied definition | Pressure on a part or a specific letter of the word, so that its sound is a little louder than the neighbouring letters.
## Positions of accent
1) When pronouncing waaw or yaa with shaddah, like: {{q ?|إِ[يَّ]اكَ}}, {{q ?|مِّن قُ[وَّ]ةٍ}}, {{q 4:135|يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ كُونُوا۟ قَ[وَّ]ٰمِينَ بِٱلْقِسْطِ}}.
2) When moving from a lengthening letter to the first letter of the mushaddad letter (in the case of compulsory lengthening), like: {{q 1:7|غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآ[لِّ]ينَ}}, {{q 80:33|فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلصَّآ[خَّ]ةُ}}, {{q ?|وَمَا مِن دَآ[بَّ]ةٍ فِى ٱلْأَرْضِ}}.
3) When stopping on a hamzah that comes after a lengthening or leen letter, like: {{q ?|ٱلسَّمَآ[ءِ]}}, {{q ?|سُوٓ[ءَ]}}, {{q ?|سِىٓ[ءَ] بِهِمْ}}.
4) When the alif of the dual is dropped to avoid the meeting of two sakin letters, like: {{q 7:22|ذَا[قَا] ٱلشَّجَرَةَ}}, {{q 27:15|وَ[قَالَا] ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ}}.
5) When stopping on a mushaddad letter, like: {{q ?|يَٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلنَّبِ[ىُّ]}}, {{q 54:3|وَكُلُّ أَمْرٍ مُّسْتَقِ[رٌّ]}}, {{q 2:255|ٱللَّهُ لَآ إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْحَ[ىُّ] ٱلْقَيُّومُ}}, {{q 31:10|أَن تَمِيدَ بِكُمْ وَبَ[ثَّ] فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ دَآبَّةٍ}}.
### Note: nabr is applied when stopping on a mushaddad letter, except
1) Stopping on noon and meem mushaddad, like: {{q ?|وَلَٰكِ[نَّ]}}, {{q ?|فِى ٱلْيَ[مِّ]}}.
2) Stopping on a letter of qalqalah with shaddah, like: {{q 111:1|وَتَ[بَّ]}}, {{q ?|بِٱلْحَ[قِّ]}}.
