# Arabic Alphabet ({{a الحُروفُ العَربيَّة}})
The Holy Qur'an consists of (surahs) chapters, the chapters consist of verses (Ayat), the verses consist of words and every word consists of letters so, the smallest unit of the Qur'an is the letter.

# Articulation Points of Letters ({{a مَخارِجُ الحُروفِ}})
- **Articulation point in lingustics**: The place of articulation.
- **In applied definition**: The place of articulation of the letter where the sound stops hence, the letter is distinguished from other letters.
- **Specific articulation point**: It depends on certain part of the throat, the tongue or the lips.
- **Approximate articulation point**: It does not depend on certain part of the throat, the tongue or the lips, It has no specific point, but it finishes by stopping of exhale (air), It can be prolonged or shortened and **three letters of madd** are emitted from this articulation point.

### How do you know the articulation point of the letter?
1) We can pronounce the letter with sukoon.
2) We admit hamzatul Wasl before the sakin letter.
3) we pronounce the letter and where the sound stops, the articulation point is established.
### Types of articulation points
There are major articulation points (**main**) and specific articulation points (**minor**). Scholars have three opinions about the number of specific articulation points (minor) that fall under the major articulation points (main).
1) **There are 17 articulation points**: The school of thought is followed by Ibn Al_Gazari and also the majority of scholars as the following: (**1**) articulation point from Jawf, (**3**) articulation points from throat, (**10**) articulation points from tongue, (**2**) articulation points from lips and (**1**) articulation point from nasal cavity.
2) **There are 16 articulation points**: The school of thought is followed by Sibawayh and A'Shatby. They do not consider Jawf as articulation point. They believe that letters that are emitted from Jawf have the same articulation point of the identical letters in drawing, Alif madd is articulated from the same articulation point of hamzah (throat), waaw madd is articulated from the same articulation point of non_lengthening waaw (lips) and Yaa madd is articulated from the same articulation point of non_lengthening yaa (the middle of the tongue).
3) **There are 14 articulation points**: The school of thought is followed by Al_Faraa and Qutrub. they agree Sybawyh and his followers on disregarding 1 articulation point from Jawf. they add also that **laam, raa and noon** are emitted from the same articulation point.

# 5 – Khayshoom (The nasal cavity) ({{a الخَيشوم}})
- The nasal cavity is the opening that connects the furthest point of the nose to the throat and Produces ghunnah.
- **Ghunnah (as a letter)**: is a sound is articulated from khayshoom (The nasal cavity) and accompanies noon and meem in all cases. Meem is not free from ghunnah; Noon is not free from ghunnah.

# Completing harakahs (vowels) ({{a إتمامُ الحركات}})
## Letter with fathah
When the reciter pronounces letter with fathah, he must open his mouth by the same way of pronouncing Alif. {{q ?|تَ[حْ]سَبُ}}.
!crop 375 106 529 261
## Letter with dammah
When the reciter pronounces letter with dammah, he must circle his lips by the same way of pronouncing Waaw. {{q ?|[سُ]لْطَٰنِيَهْ}}.
!crop 377 279 525 429
## Letter with kasrah
When the reciter pronounces letter with kasrah, he must drop his lower jaw and raise the middle of the tongue by the same way of pronouncing Yaa. {{q ?|أُقْ[سِ]مُ}}.
!crop 378 460 522 609
## Letter with Sukoon
Letter with sukoon is emitted from its original articulation point without any of the above. {{q ?|إِ[سْ]رَارًا}}.
