# Definition of the Science of Tajweed ({{a تَعرِيفُ عِلْمِ التَّجويد}})
### Its definition
= Tajweed in lingustics | Enhancement.
= In applied definition | A science is established to give every letter its right and due from the articulation point and characteristic, when stopping or starting with no exaggration or intensiveness.
- **Its right**: inherent, permanent and constant characteristics that cannot be removed from the letter like Jahr (appearence) and Shiddah (Strength).
- **Its due**: Temporary characteristics are caused by permanent characteristics like Idgham (merging), ikhfaa (hiding) and Ithhar (clarity).... etc.
### Its rule
= Theoretical aspect | To recognize the rules, regulations and conditions of tajweed. **Its rule**: Fard Kfayah (Collective duty).
= Practical aspect | To apply theoretical rules of tajweed during the recitation of the Qur'an. **Its rule**: Fard Ain (an individual duty).
### Its advantage and benefit
- To keep your tongue from committing mistakes (Lahn) when reciting the Qur'an.
- To get Allah's pleasure.

### You can acquire this knowledge (Science of Tajweed) by 2 ways
1) **Arriwayah (narration)**: By Ard or Talqeen (Show or Repetition) or by both two matters. The meaning of **Show**: The student recites to Sheikh. The meaning of **Repetition**: Sheikh recites before the student, then the student repeates all were recited by the sheikh.
2) **Addirayah (knowledge)**: To study and understand the theoretical rules of tajweed and apply them to the verses of the Qur'an, If the reciter forgot some of them, he should review the rule and correct his recitation.
- He must recite to a skillful Sheikh to improve performance and gather between the two ways (**Riwayah and Dirayah**).
