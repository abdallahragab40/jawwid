# Sujood at-tilawah (prostration of recitation) ({{a سُجودُ التِّلاوة}})
It is a prostration performed after reciting a verse of prostration.
= Its rule | A stressed sunnah (sunnah mu'akkadah): the Prophet Mohammed (peace be upon him) performed it continuously and almost never abandoned it.
= Its positions | There are 15 positions of prostration of recitation: [Al-A'raf: 206], [Ar-Ra'd: 15], [An-Nahl: 49], [Al-Isra': 107], [Maryam: 58], [Al-Hajj: 18], [Al-Hajj: 77], [Al-Furqan: 60], [An-Naml: 25], [As-Sajdah: 15], [Sad: 24], [Fussilat: 37], [An-Najm: 62], [Al-Inshiqaq: 21], [Al-'Alaq: 19].
= What is said during it | It is sunnah to say during the prostration of recitation: "{{a سَجَدَ وجهيَ للذي خلقهُ، وشقَّ سَمعهُ وبَصرهُ، بِحولِه وقُوَّتِه}}" — "My face has prostrated to the one who created it and brought forth its hearing and sight by his might and power" Sahih A'Tirmidhi 3425 Some scholars allowed to say "{{a سبحانَ ربيَ الأعلى}}" — "Glory be to my lord, the most high" or do like all the positions of prostration.
