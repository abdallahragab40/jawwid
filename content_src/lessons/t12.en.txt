# Noon and Meem Mushaddad ({{a النُّونُ والمِيمُ المُشدَّدَتين}})
### What must be known
- **Ghunnah** ({{a الغُنَّة}}): a sound emitted from the nasal cavity accompanying **noon** and **meem** in all cases.
- When the reciter pronounces noon or meem mushaddad, he must lengthen the timing of ghunnah (most complete ghunnah) in continuing recitation or stopping.
### Examples
- {{q ?|مِنَ ٱلْجِ[نَّ]ةِ وَٱل[نَّ]اسِ}}, {{q 8:43|وَلَٰكِ[نَّ] ٱللَّهَ سَلَّمَ}}, {{q 36:68|وَمَن [نُّ]عَمِّرْهُ}}.
- {{q ?|حَ[مَّ]الَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ}}, {{q ?|ثُ[مَّ] كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ}}, {{q ?|مِن [مَّ]الٍ وَبَنِينَ}}.
- {{q 2:124|وَإِذِ ٱبْتَلَىٰٓ إِبْرَٰهِۦمَ رَبُّهُۥ بِكَلِمَٰتٍ فَأَتَ[مَّ]هُ[نَّ]}}.
- {{q ?|إِ[نَّ] ٱللَّهَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ قَدِيرٌ}}.
### Note
If we want to start with {{q 36:68|[نُّ]عَمِّرْهُ}} or {{q ?|[مَّ]الٍ}} In the previous examples, we omit shaddah and Start with meem and noon with no excessive ghunnah, shaddah also comes from Idgham and when we start with these letters, there is no Idgham.
