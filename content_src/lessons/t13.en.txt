# Timings of ghunnah ({{a أزمِنةُ الغُنَن}})
1) **Most complete ghunnah**: With noon and meem mushaddad, With merged noon and meem in the case of Idgham with ghunnah or Idgham shafawi Like: {{q 8:43|وَلَٰكِ[نَّ] ٱللَّهَ سَلَّمَ}}, {{q 28:7|فِى ٱلْيَ[مِّ] وَلَا تَخَافِى}}, {{q ?|مَلِكِ ٱل[نَّ]اسِ}}, {{q 87:9|إِ[ن نَّ]فَعَتِ}}, {{q ?|وَمَ[ن يَ]عْمَلْ}}, {{q 10:27|مَّا لَهُ[م مِّ]نَ ٱللَّهِ}}.
2) **Complete ghunnah**: With hidden noon and meem in the case of Ikhfaa Haqiqy, ikhfaa shafawi and Iqlab like: {{q ?|ٱلْإِ[ن]سَٰنُ}}, {{q ?|وَمَن يَعْتَصِ[م بِ]ٱللَّهِ}}, {{q ?|مِ[نۢ بَ]عْدِ}}.
3) **Incomplete ghunnah**: with noon and meem sakinah when they are recited with Ithhar like: {{q ?|أَ[نْ]عَمْتَ}}, {{q ?|حَكِي[مٌ عَ]لِيمٌ}}, {{q ?|وَهُ[مْ فَ]رِحُونَ}}.
4) **Most incomplete ghunnah**: with noon and meem with vowels like: {{q 2:93|بِئْسَ[مَ]ا يَأْ[مُ]رُكُم بِهِۦٓ إِي[مَٰنُ]كُمْ إِن كُنتُم مُّؤْ[مِنِ]ينَ}}.
