# The chain of transmission of the narration of Hafs ({{a إسنادُ روايةِ حفصٍ}})
The chain, from top to bottom:
- **The Prophet Muhammad** (peace be upon him)
- **'Uthman ibn 'Affan**, **'Ali ibn Abi Talib**, **'Abdullah ibn Mas'ud**, **Ubayy ibn Ka'b**, **Zaid ibn Thabit**
- **'Abdullah ibn Habib**
- **'Asim**
- **Hafs**
> **Notice**: The narration of Hafs is the most widespread narration in the Islamic world.
