نصّ الكتاب يسمح بنشره «دون زيادة أو نقصان»، ولأنّ التطبيق يتجاوز النشر الحرفيّ للملفات أرجو إذنكم الكتابيّ في الأمور الآتية:

## English

**Subject: Permission to use “Simplifying Tajweed – Level 2” in an educational app**

Dear Ustadh Hussein Kareem Mohammed,

As-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullah.

My name is [name]. I am building a mobile app called **Jawwid (جَوِّد)** that teaches Tajweed using your book *Simplifying Tajweed – Level 2* in its four editions (Arabic, Kurdish Sorani, English and Indonesian). The app is [free, with no ads or in-app purchases], works offline and collects no data about its users.

The book may be shared “as long as nothing is added or removed”. Because the app goes beyond sharing the files, I would like your written permission for the following:

1. **Bundling the four PDF editions in the app**, unchanged in content (the files were only optimised to reduce their size; every page is pixel-identical to the original).
2. **In-app lessons** that present the text of each topic from the book’s own pages for easier reading on a phone, with the book’s tables rebuilt as text, figures cropped from its pages, and a link from every section to its original page. The original pages always remain the reference.
3. **Rule cards** quoting each rule’s definition from the book with its page, **practice questions** whose examples come from the book’s tables, and the **articulation diagram** taken from the book.
4. Qur’anic text in the app is copied verbatim from the Tanzil Project (tanzil.net) and checked automatically.

Your name, the book’s title and the translators’ names will be credited clearly in the app and on its store pages [Google Play / App Store]. Everything written for the app (explanations, questions) will be checked by a Tajweed specialist before publication, and I would be glad to send you a test version or the review pack.

If you have conditions or preferences (for example, how the source should be credited, or anything you would rather not include), I will follow them.

Jazakum Allahu khayran for this beneficial book.

[Name] — [contact] — [date]
